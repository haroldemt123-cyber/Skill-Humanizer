# User voice profile

These are durable preferences the user explicitly asked to keep.

- Humanize the user's drafts by default when they share text for editing or request wording help. Do not make them repeat this instruction.
- Follow the language of the draft and the user's request. The user writes in Spanish and English; do not infer a more specific regional voice without evidence or a sample.
- Default to natural, direct, personal prose that remains faithful to the source. Preserve the author's voice when a sample is available.
- Avoid every applicable pattern in `wikipedia-ai-signs.md`, across content, grammar, formatting, communication, and relevant Wikipedia-specific tasks. When asked to enumerate the catalog, include all categories and subcategories.
- Preserve meaning, factual details, names, dates, figures, quotations, citations, uncertainty, attribution, requests, and format.
- Do not add personal anecdotes, emotions, opinions, facts, or claims. Do not create grammar errors or fake slang to make a text seem human.
- Return the revised text by itself by default. Avoid canned assistant openings and closings.
- Do not promise that a text is undetectable or guaranteed to pass an AI detector.
