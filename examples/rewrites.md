# Humanization Examples (Before and After)

These examples show practical transformations from texts full of AI patterns to human, natural, and direct versions. They serve to calibrate how much to rewrite. Notice three things: the new text does not add data, it cuts what said nothing, and it leaves a question where the person's own detail would improve things. The "before" texts are intentionally crafted with the catalog's patterns.

---

## Example 1: Professional email / work coordination

### Before (typical AI text):
> **Subject:** Project update and crucial information about next steps
>
> Dear team:
>
> I hope this message finds you very well. I am reaching out to share a fundamental update regarding the evolution of our project.
>
> During the past weeks, we have navigated a dynamic and complex landscape. It is a testament to the team's dedication that we have achieved advances not only in process optimization, but also in strengthening our internal collaboration.
>
> Below, I present a more detailed breakdown of the key points:
> * **Efficiency:** We managed to reduce delivery times meticulously.
> * **Innovation:** We fostered new solutions that align with our strategic vision.
> * **Impact:** We laid the groundwork for addressing future challenges with resilience.
>
> In summary, we are well-positioned for the future. If you have any questions or suggestions, do not hesitate to let me know. I remain at your complete disposal.
>
> Best regards,

### After (human, direct, and real):
> **Subject:** Project progress and this week's pending items
>
> Hi everyone,
>
> Quick summary of where we are with the project and what we need to sort out this week.
>
> In the past two weeks we managed to clear the delivery backlog and reorganize shifts with the support team. Switching the tracking tool helped a lot: response times dropped nearly 30%.
>
> What's still pending for this Friday:
> 1. Finish testing the mobile version with the test users.
> 2. Decide who's on call for the long weekend.
>
> If you see anything else that needs adjusting before Friday, let me know here or we'll cover it in tomorrow's call.
>
> Cheers,

## Example 2: Explanatory / educational text

### Before (typical AI text):
> In today's technological landscape, quantum computing stands as a beacon of unprecedented innovation. It is not just a computational advance; it is a paradigm shift that will transform entire industries, from biomedicine to global finance.
>
> Delving into this intricate field, the crucial role of qubits stands out, which leverage the principles of superposition and entanglement. This fascinating phenomenon allows processing massive volumes of data, laying the groundwork for revolutionary discoveries, thus facilitating a continuous research environment.
>
> Despite its immense potential, the discipline faces several challenges, particularly around stability and error correction. In conclusion, the journey toward quantum supremacy will require vision, resilience, and continuous interdisciplinary collaboration.

### After (human, without inflating or pontificating):
> Quantum computers work with different logic than traditional ones. Instead of processing information through classical bits (which can only be 0 or 1), they use qubits, which thanks to quantum physics properties like superposition can represent combinations of both states at the same time.
>
> In practice, this would let them solve in minutes calculations that would take a current supercomputer years, like simulating the behavior of complex molecules for designing new drugs or cracking certain types of encryption.
>
> The big obstacle right now is stability. Qubits are extremely sensitive to heat and environmental interference, which causes continuous errors in calculations. Until reliable error correction systems or processors that work without extreme cold are designed, the technology will stay confined to research labs.

## Example 3: Professional profile / "About Me"

### Before (typical AI text):
> ✨ **About Me**
>
> Hi! I am a passionate software developer with a rich and diverse trajectory in creating innovative, scalable, and efficient solutions. Throughout my career, I have demonstrated an unwavering commitment to technical excellence, navigating complex development environments and fostering a culture of continuous learning.
>
> My holistic approach encompasses:
> * 🚀 **Backend Development:** Meticulously designed to optimize performance.
> * 💡 **Cloud Architecture:** Leveraging cutting-edge technologies to ensure robustness.
> * 🤝 **Collaboration:** Driving team success through open communication.
>
> Let's connect and explore new opportunities together!

### After (human, specific, and without posing):
> I've been working as a backend developer for about eight years, mostly with Python and Go in cloud environments (AWS and GCP).
>
> Most of my recent experience has been at e-commerce and payments companies, where I've been in charge of redesigning APIs to handle traffic spikes and migrating monolithic databases to distributed schemas. I'm particularly interested in database performance and critical service monitoring.
>
> When I'm not writing code, I contribute to a couple of open-source libraries and usually write technical notes about specific problems I run into in production. If you want to discuss a project or talk tech, you can reach me on LinkedIn or email.

## Example 4: Forum or technical debate response

### Before (typical AI text):
> Dear thread participants:
>
> I sincerely appreciate the valuable perspectives shared in this discussion. I would like to point out with due respect that my previous interventions strictly align with community best practices and have as their sole purpose contributing clarity and value to the debate.
>
> I understand the concerns expressed regarding the framework choice. However, I suggest we focus on the substantive aspects of the code rather than unfounded speculation about contributors' intentions. If there are concrete observations about specific lines, I will be happy to review them meticulously to ensure the repository meets the highest quality standards.
>
> Best regards,

### After (human, natural, and to the point):
> I get the question about why we used that library instead of the standard option. The main reason was time: it already came with the auth adapters we needed for this sprint and saved us from building the entire session layer from scratch.
>
> That said, if you think it'll cause dependency or maintenance headaches down the road, I have no problem redoing that module with the native solution. Is there a specific use case where you think it'll bottleneck? Let's go over it in the pull request.

## Example 5: School paper paragraph

### Before:
> Rural transportation plays a crucial role in the marketing of agricultural products, as it not only facilitates access to markets, but also contributes to the economic development of communities. Additionally, road deficiency represents a significant challenge that affects producers, transporters, and consumers.

### After:
> Rural transportation decides whether what's produced in the countryside reaches the market. When roads are in poor condition, the problem hits producers, transporters, and buyers.

Note for the person: a detail of yours (a specific community, a distance, how long a bus ride takes) would make this paragraph more yours.

Patterns removed: "crucial" role, "not only... but also", "Additionally", "significant challenge", importance gerund, trio of actors without function.

## Example 6: Email to a professor

### Before:
> Dear Professor, I hope you are very well. I am writing to you in order to request, with due respect, an extension for the assignment submission, as I have not only had technical difficulties, but also organizational challenges.

### After:
> Professor, good afternoon. Could I have a few more days to submit the paper? I had computer problems and lost track of time.

The respectful tone was maintained, as the context requires it, and both reasons the person gave were preserved. No date was invented.

## Example 7: Sentence with no content

### Before:
> In today's ever-evolving world, technology has become a fundamental tool that transforms the way we live, work, and communicate.

### After:
> (The sentence is removed.)

Note for the person: this sentence doesn't say anything that's yours, so I cut it. If you wanted to talk about something specific about technology (a use, a problem, something you saw), tell me what it is and I'll write from there.

When a sentence only inflates, cutting is better than rewriting. Turning it into another ornamental sentence only changes the symptoms.

## Example 8: Text that already sounds human

### Before:
> My grandfather sold bananas at the market. He left at four in the morning. Sometimes the bus didn't come and he had to wait until noon.

### After:
> (No changes.)

There are specific details, sentences of different lengths, and no formulas. Touching it would make it worse. The Wikipedia guide warns that clean grammar or isolated connectors prove nothing.

## Example 9: Text copied from a chat

### Before:
> Certainly! Here's a draft for your essay:
>
> **Key Points:**
> - **Climate:** The island boasts a vibrant ecosystem.
> - **Economy:** Agriculture serves as a cornerstone. [oai_citation:0‡source.com]
>
> I hope this helps! Let me know if you'd like changes.

### After:
> (Rewritten as running prose, using only the data the person provides. If the person provided no data, they are asked for the real data before writing anything.)

Patterns: chatbot opening and closing, bold and bullet points with header, "boasts a vibrant", "serves as a cornerstone", and a citation marker residue. Any fact from the chat without a source is verified or discarded.
